I want you to refine the UX/UI of the EXISTING VC investment intelligence application.

IMPORTANT:
- DO NOT rebuild the application.
- DO NOT change the underlying product architecture.
- DO NOT change the existing five-stage workflow.
- DO NOT remove or rewrite the existing intelligence, analysis, scoring logic, data models, or backend functionality.
- This is primarily a UX/UI refinement.
- Preserve the existing functionality and improve how the information is presented and understood.
- The goal is to make the product feel like a brilliant VC associate sitting beside the investor, not like an AI dashboard.

The existing workflow is:

1. SOURCE
2. TRIAGE
3. VALIDATION
4. PROCESS
5. DECISION

PROCESS is an operational/control layer and can run alongside the analytical workflow. Do not treat it as a rigid sequential stage.

==================================================
1. CORE UX PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The current application exposes too much internal system architecture.

The investor should see the INVESTMENT STORY, not the software architecture.

The interface should answer immediately:

- Where are we?
- What happened?
- What did we learn?
- What concerns us?
- What do we still need?
- Who needs to act?
- What happens next?

A deal should be understandable within 5–10 seconds.

Use progressive disclosure:

LEVEL 1:
What matters?

LEVEL 2:
Why does it matter?

LEVEL 3:
Show the evidence, calculation, source, or detailed reasoning.

Do not put every piece of information on the first screen.

==================================================
2. VISUAL DESIGN
==================================================

Make the UI:

- simple
- clean
- minimal
- premium
- institutional
- highly readable
- spacious
- restrained

Avoid:

- excessive cards
- giant metric widgets
- unnecessary badges
- excessive icons
- decorative charts
- gradients
- neon colors
- oversized headings
- dashboard clutter
- too many competing visual elements

Use typography, spacing, alignment, subtle borders, and hierarchy to create structure.

The product should feel closer to a premium institutional investment tool than a generic SaaS dashboard.

==================================================
3. DEAL HEADER
==================================================

The top of each deal should immediately communicate:

Company
Current Stage
Investment Conviction
Risk
Evidence Confidence
Next Best Action

Do not display a wall of metrics.

The header should be extremely easy to scan.

==================================================
4. THREE PRIMARY VISUAL METERS
==================================================

Do NOT use qualitative text labels as the primary representation of the three top-level signals.

Instead, represent them using compact pictorial visualizations similar to elegant speedometers / semicircular gauges.

The three primary visual signals are:

1. INVESTMENT CONVICTION
2. RISK
3. EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE

These should be compact and visually intuitive.

The underlying numerical score remains in the backend.

The number can be shown subtly, but the visual meter is the PRIMARY representation.

The investor should be able to understand the situation visually without interpreting a dashboard full of numbers.

--------------------------------------------------
INVESTMENT CONVICTION
--------------------------------------------------

Use a compact semicircular gauge / speedometer.

The gauge should visually communicate movement from weak → strong.

The exact underlying score can remain numerical.

Example:

Investment Conviction
[semicircular gauge]
72
↑ +8 since last review

Clicking / expanding the gauge should show:

- current score
- previous score
- what changed
- why it changed
- supporting evidence

Important:
Investment Conviction is NOT the probability that the investor will invest.

It is the synthesized assessment of how compelling the investment currently is given the opportunity, evidence, risks, valuation, thesis fit, and portfolio considerations.

--------------------------------------------------
RISK
--------------------------------------------------

Use a separate compact visual gauge.

The gauge should visually communicate:

Low → Higher Risk

Example:

Risk
[semicircular gauge]
Elevated

Primary concern:
Financial reporting inconsistency

Do not rely on color alone.

--------------------------------------------------
EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Use a third compact visual gauge.

The gauge should visually communicate:

Weak Evidence → Strong Evidence

Example:

Evidence Confidence
[semicircular gauge]
Moderate

This is important because opportunity quality and evidence quality are different things.

For example:

Investment Conviction = Strong
Evidence Confidence = Low

The system should make it immediately clear that:

"The company looks attractive, but the evidence base is still incomplete."

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT GAUGE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

DO NOT create visual gauges for every metric.

Only use these three top-level visual meters:

- Investment Conviction
- Risk
- Evidence Confidence

Other dimensions should remain simple supporting indicators or rows:

- Opportunity Quality
- Thesis Fit
- Valuation Attractiveness
- Portfolio Fit
- Market
- Product
- Team
- Financials

These can be expanded for detail.

==================================================
5. CURRENT VIEW
==================================================

Near the top of each deal, create a concise AI-generated:

CURRENT VIEW

This should explain the current investment case in plain English.

It should answer:

- Why do we like this company?
- What concerns us?
- What evidence is driving our current view?
- What remains unresolved?
- What should happen next?

This should read like a strong VC associate briefing a partner.

Do not make it a long AI essay.

Keep it concise and decision-oriented.

==================================================
6. NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION
==================================================

Create a highly focused:

NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION

section.

Only show the highest-priority actions.

Examples:

Founder clarification needed on revenue discrepancy
Financial model requires validation
Customer evidence still pending
Next partner meeting tomorrow

Each item should clearly communicate:

WHAT
WHY
WHO NEEDS TO ACT
NEXT ACTION

Do not show every open task here.

==================================================
7. WHAT CHANGED
==================================================

Create:

WHAT CHANGED SINCE YOUR LAST VISIT

Only show MATERIAL changes.

Examples:

+ Revenue definition clarified
+ 3 financial discrepancies resolved
+ Customer retention independently validated
- Market estimate remains unsupported

Do not show trivial system activity.

The investor should immediately understand what changed in the investment picture.

==================================================
8. DEAL STORY / TIMELINE
==================================================

Create a clear chronological DEAL STORY.

Every meaningful activity must explicitly identify:

WHO
WHAT
WHEN
WHY / IMPACT

Use explicit actor labels:

YOU / INVESTMENT TEAM
FOUNDER
AGENT
EXTERNAL

Examples:

AGENT
Found 4 inconsistencies in financial reporting.

YOU
Requested clarification from Founder.

FOUNDER
Uploaded revised financial model.

AGENT
Re-ran financial validation.

AGENT
3 discrepancies resolved; 1 remains unresolved.

Do NOT use vague activity labels such as:

"Reply applied"
"Analysis completed"
"Conviction updated"

The timeline should tell the story of the deal.

==================================================
9. FOUNDER VS INVESTOR COMMUNICATION
==================================================

Make the distinction between internal investor activity and founder communication extremely obvious.

There are three separate concepts:

FINDING
What the system detected.

QUESTION
What needs to be answered.

FOUNDER MESSAGE
The actual external communication.

Show the relationship clearly:

Finding
↓
Question
↓
Founder Message
↓
Founder Response
↓
Updated Finding

Do not mix internal notes with external founder communication.

External messages should NOT be automatically sent by default.

The agent should draft the message.

The investor should control sending:

[EDIT]
[SEND TO FOUNDER]

==================================================
10. DISCREPANCIES
==================================================

When multiple discrepancies exist in the same document, DO NOT send separate communications for each discrepancy.

Detect ALL material discrepancies.

Show ALL discrepancies individually.

Then GROUP related discrepancies under a common underlying issue.

Example:

UNDERLYING ISSUE
Financial reporting inconsistency

Individual findings:

Revenue
Deck: $4.2M
Financial model: $2.8M

Runway
Deck: 18 months
Financial model: 9 months

Headcount
Deck: 40
Financial model: 27

Founder ownership
Deck: 15%
Cap table: 8.4%

This hierarchy should be:

Underlying Issue
→ Related Findings
→ Individual Discrepancies
→ Evidence

Grouping should simplify the story WITHOUT hiding the individual discrepancies.

==================================================
11. CONSOLIDATED FOUNDER REQUEST
==================================================

After detecting discrepancies:

1. Detect all discrepancies
2. Group related discrepancies
3. Assess materiality
4. Determine which require founder clarification
5. Draft ONE consolidated founder request
6. Allow investor to edit
7. Investor sends
8. Map the founder response back to each individual finding
9. Re-run only relevant analyses

Example:

FOUNDER RESPONSE

Revenue discrepancy
Partially resolved

Runway discrepancy
Still open

Headcount discrepancy
Resolved

Ownership discrepancy
Resolved

Do not treat one founder response as resolving an entire group automatically.

Each finding must retain its own status.

==================================================
12. PENDING ITEMS VS OPEN QUESTIONS
==================================================

Keep these concepts distinct.

PENDING ITEM =
Something we need to RECEIVE.

Examples:
Updated financial model
Customer cohort data
Cap table
Contract

OPEN QUESTION =
Something we need ANSWERED.

Examples:
Why does revenue differ?
What explains the churn increase?
How was the market size estimated?

These should not be mixed.

"Waiting" is a STATUS, not a stage.

==================================================
13. NEW EVIDENCE
==================================================

When new evidence arrives, the system should automatically identify what analyses are affected.

For example:

Founder uploads revised financial model.

The system should:

- identify relevant discrepancies
- update evidence
- re-run relevant validations
- update affected findings
- update risk where appropriate
- update uncertainty where appropriate
- update evidence confidence
- update investment conviction when justified
- create a clear "What Changed" entry

Do not blindly re-run everything.

Re-analyze only what the new evidence can actually affect.

==================================================
14. VALIDATION
==================================================

Validation should combine:

- financial validation
- market validation
- customer validation
- competition
- product
- team
- external reality checks
- quantitative plausibility
- benchmarking
- evidence verification

The purpose is not simply to summarize the pitch.

The purpose is to determine:

"What appears true?"
"What is supported?"
"What is inconsistent?"
"What is plausible?"
"What remains uncertain?"

==================================================
15. QUANTITATIVE VALIDATION
==================================================

Material quantitative claims should be independently checked.

Examples:

- Total Market Size
- Reachable Market Size
- revenue forecasts
- customer counts
- growth assumptions
- margins
- runway
- valuation
- return scenarios

For important claims compare:

1. Management value
2. Supporting source data
3. Recomputed value
4. External benchmark
5. Comparable companies
6. Historical trend
7. Agent estimate
8. Reasonable range
9. Confidence

Clearly distinguish:

FACT
MANAGEMENT ASSUMPTION
EXTERNAL DATA
AGENT ESTIMATE
SCENARIO
ASSUMPTION

Do NOT fabricate statistical certainty.

Use percentile/range language such as 80th or 90th percentile only when there is an actual defensible underlying distribution, benchmark, model, or scenario basis.

==================================================
16. CORE VALIDATION CHECKS
==================================================

Retain these core checks:

1. Claim-to-evidence verification
2. Cross-document contradiction detection
3. Stated-vs-recomputed values
4. Historical-vs-forecast consistency
5. Population completeness

Every material finding should be traceable to:

Claim
→ Evidence
→ Source
→ Document / Page

Do not treat the pitch deck as equivalent to primary evidence.

==================================================
17. RISK
==================================================

Risk should NOT be a single arbitrary score that determines the investment decision.

Separate:

- Risk
- Uncertainty
- Evidence Confidence
- Opportunity Quality
- Thesis Fit
- Valuation Attractiveness
- Portfolio Fit
- Investment Conviction

Avoid double-counting correlated risks.

For example, four symptoms of the same underlying financial reporting problem should not become four independent risk penalties.

==================================================
18. DECISION ROOM
==================================================

The DECISION stage should feel like a true:

DECISION ROOM

not a static AI report.

Top of Decision Room:

Company
Investment Conviction gauge
Risk gauge
Evidence Confidence gauge

Then:

CURRENT VIEW

WHY WE LIKE IT

WHAT CONCERNS US

WHAT WE DON'T KNOW

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR VIEW

NEXT BEST ACTION

The investor should be able to understand the decision case without opening every analytical section.

==================================================
19. NEXT BEST ACTION
==================================================

Always prioritize ONE primary next action.

Example:

NEXT BEST ACTION

Validate customer retention data before advancing to Investment Committee review.

Why this matters:
Current conviction is being limited by uncertainty around retention.

Secondary actions can be expanded.

Do not show ten equally weighted recommendations.

==================================================
20. INTERACTIVE ANALYST
==================================================

The interactive analyst is an INTERNAL VC analytical assistant.

It is not a generic chatbot.

It should answer questions using the deal's evidence, analysis, history, and current state.

Useful suggested prompts:

"Why is conviction at this level?"
"What concerns us most?"
"Challenge the investment case."
"What assumptions are most aggressive?"
"What would change our view?"
"Show me the downside case."
"Compare this with my other deals."
"What should I investigate next?"

Responses should clearly distinguish:

Verified evidence
Inference
Assumption
Scenario
Uncertainty

Never make an AI interpretation appear to be a verified fact.

==================================================
21. THESIS FIT
==================================================

The investor's thesis is a LENS / PRIOR, not an automatic rejection gate.

The system should determine:

- Strong thesis fit
- Moderate thesis fit
- Weak thesis fit
- Outside normal investment preference

But an unusual company should NOT automatically be rejected.

Instead, flag it as a potential:

"Thesis Exception"

and continue evaluating the actual investment opportunity.

==================================================
22. PLAIN LANGUAGE
==================================================

Use plain English throughout the product.

Avoid unnecessary acronyms.

Use:

Total Market Size
Reachable Market Size
Annual Recurring Revenue
Customer Acquisition Cost
Customer Lifetime Value
Investment Committee

instead of unexplained acronyms.

The interface should be understandable without requiring the investor to decode terminology.

==================================================
23. HOMEPAGE / MAIN DASHBOARD
==================================================

The homepage should prioritize ACTION rather than metrics.

Primary sections:

NEEDS YOUR ATTENTION

READY FOR REVIEW

WAITING

RECENTLY UPDATED

A deal row should NOT contain ten metrics.

Show only:

Company
Stage
Investment Conviction visual signal
Risk visual signal
Next Action

Everything else can be expanded.

==================================================
24. ACTOR SYSTEM
==================================================

Every meaningful activity must identify who performed it.

Use:

YOU / INVESTMENT TEAM
FOUNDER
AGENT
EXTERNAL

This should be consistent across:

Timeline
Findings
Questions
Tasks
Communications
Evidence
Decision updates

The investor should never be confused about whether something was:

- discovered by the agent
- requested by the investor
- provided by the founder
- sourced externally

25. FINAL UX STANDARD
==================================================

The application should feel like:

"A brilliant VC associate sitting beside me and keeping the entire
investment process organized, analytical, evidence-based, and

"An AI dashboard showing me every internal system state."

Prioritize:

Clarity
Hierarchy
Chronology
Actionability
Evidence
Context
Decision support

Over:

More metrics
More cards

WHAT WORRIES US?

Make these UX changes without rebuilding or changing the underlying
application intelligence.
