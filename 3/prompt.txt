BUILD A FULL-STACK VC INVESTMENT INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM.

The product is for a VC who manages multiple startup deals simultaneously.

The VC has a configurable investment thesis, but thesis alignment must NEVER be used as an automatic rejection gate. A startup outside the preferred thesis can still become an attractive investment. The system should flag a thesis mismatch or thesis exception and continue evaluating the opportunity.

The product must model the complete deal lifecycle using these 9 stages:

1. SOURCE
2. TRIAGE
3. PROCESS
4. NUMBERS
5. WORLD
6. PEOPLE + PRODUCT
7. FOUNDER LOOP
8. IC
9. CLOSE / PASS

The nine stages are the user-facing workflow layer.

The analytical engines operate continuously across stages.

CORE PRODUCT LOOP:

Deal enters
→ stage assigned
→ documents / meetings / data added
→ claims extracted
→ evidence linked
→ checks run
→ market / benchmark analysis
→ risk updated
→ uncertainty updated
→ thesis fit updated
→ opportunity quality updated
→ valuation updated
→ investment conviction updated
→ next best action generated
→ new information arrives
→ relevant analyses rerun
→ investment case evolves.

--------------------------------

Allow the investor to define:

- Preferred sectors
- Avoided sectors

- Check-size range
- Business model preferences
- Founder preferences
- Market preferences
- Growth preferences
- Capital-efficiency preferences
- Hard constraints
- Soft preferences

Store all thesis settings historically.

5. TRIAGE
--------------------------------

Do not make a final investment decision.

Analyze:

- Initial thesis fit
- Initial market attractiveness
- Founder signals
- Initial traction
- Raise
- Obvious risks

Output:

- Initial opportunity score
- Thesis fit
- Initial risk
- Evidence confidence
- Recommended next step

Possible outcomes:

- Take meeting
- Request information
- Watch
- Decline

--------------------------------
6. PROCESS
--------------------------------

Track the operational workflow:

- Meetings
- Owners
- Tasks
- Questions
- Document requests
- Deadlines

- Next meeting
- Stage changes

Show exactly what the VC is waiting for and who owns each item.

--------------------------------
7. NUMBERS
--------------------------------

Implement five core diligence checks:

1. Claim-to-evidence verification
2. Cross-document contradiction detection
3. Stated-vs-recomputed
4. Forward-vs-backward consistency
5. Population completeness

In addition implement a Plausibility / Benchmark Engine.

For every material quantitative claim such as:

- TAM
- SAM
- customer count
- ACV
- ARR
- revenue
- growth
- margin
- CAC
- churn
- retention
- market share
- forecast

compare:

- Management value
- Raw supporting data
- Recomputed value
- External benchmark
- Comparable companies
- Historical trend
- Reasonable range
- Scenario distribution

Never invent statistical certainty.

If enough evidence exists, produce a distribution/range.

Example:

Management:
$30M revenue forecast

Agent base case:
$19M

80% range:
$14M–$24M

90% range:
$11M–$27M

Assessment:
Management forecast is above the 90th percentile.

Clearly distinguish:

- Fact
- Management assumption
- External benchmark
- Agent estimate
- Scenario
